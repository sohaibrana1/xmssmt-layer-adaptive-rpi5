# JCEN Revision Reproducibility Update — 20 September 2026

This update contains the revised full-cycle Raspberry Pi 5 evaluation supporting:

**Layer-Adaptive XMSSMT Parameterization: A Multi-Objective Study of WOTS Placement, Tree-Height Allocation, and Serialized State**

## Revised experimental design

The study evaluates 20 experimental two-layer XMSSMT configurations with:

- total height H = 20
- d = 2
- BDS parameter k = 0

Height allocations:

- (6,14)
- (8,12)
- (10,10)
- (12,8)
- (14,6)

Ordered WOTS placements:

- (16,16)
- (16,4)
- (4,16)
- (4,4)

The revised campaign measures one complete lower-layer traversal cycle plus the first signature after rollover for every configuration.

Total signing observations: **87,316**.

## Interpretation safeguards

The heterogeneous XMSSMT configurations evaluated here are experimental and are not standardized parameter sets or standardized OIDs.

Logical hash-operation counters are algorithm-level work counts and are not SHA-256 compression-function counts.

The aggregate CPU-cycle model is an empirical platform/workload calibration and should not be interpreted as a primitive hash-cost model.
