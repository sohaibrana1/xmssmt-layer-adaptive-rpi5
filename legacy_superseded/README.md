# Superseded data (not used in the manuscript)
These files come from an earlier exploratory campaign that used a different
signing-window protocol. They are superseded by the matched full-cycle
campaign and are not used in any result, table, or figure of the manuscript.

Note: local absolute paths in the `source_csv` columns of
JOINT_20CELL_NORMALIZED_OPERATIONS.csv and JOINT_20CELL_SOURCE_MAP.csv were
replaced with relative paths before publication. The hashes recorded for those
two files in STAGE7L_SHA256_MANIFEST.txt therefore refer to the pre-scrub
versions.
