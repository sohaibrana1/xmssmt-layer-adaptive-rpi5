H14 ANOMALY RESOLUTION
======================

Original observation
--------------------
At h0=14, changing only upper-layer w1 from 16 to 4 produced:

w0=16:
  cycle delta = +10.0078%

w0=4:
  cycle delta = +10.3045%

The corresponding logical-hash counts were essentially unchanged.

Randomized validation
---------------------
Four h0=14 configurations were rerun in three independent randomized
orders using the same unified runtime-configurable executable.

Each cell used:
  - 10 key generations
  - 16,385 signatures
  - 16,385 verifications
  - 16,385 verification passes
  - CPU 3
  - performance governor
  - CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW
  - PMU cycle counting
  - throttling gate = 0x0

Unified executable SHA-256:

fcc98259601c07540cce74b52850eb61ceaf6db9be49b83be223bc232730d83d

Randomized w1=4 versus w1=16 cycle effects
-----------------------------------------

w0=16:
  Repeat 01 = +0.1039%
  Repeat 02 = -0.0569%
  Repeat 03 = +1.0681%
  Mean      = +0.372%

w0=4:
  Repeat 01 = +1.3330%
  Repeat 02 = -0.1275%
  Repeat 03 = +0.1956%
  Mean      = +0.467%

Interpretation
--------------
The approximately 10% effect observed in the original campaign was not
reproduced under randomized repetition.

The randomized measurements show no persistent approximately 10%
upper-layer-w effect at h0=14. The remaining differences are small,
change sign in some repetitions, and occur while mean logical work is
essentially unchanged.

Accordingly, the original approximately 10% difference should not be
interpreted as an algorithmic consequence of changing w1.

The randomized validation results will be retained as sensitivity and
provenance evidence.
