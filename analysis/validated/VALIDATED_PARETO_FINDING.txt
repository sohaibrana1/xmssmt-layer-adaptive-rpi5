Robust-dominance sensitivity
----------------------------
For the margin-based sensitivity analysis, configuration A robustly
dominates B at margin epsilon only when A improves every minimized
objective by at least that margin:

  A_m * (1 + epsilon) <= B_m

for every objective m.

Number of nondominated configurations:

                  Strict   2%   5%   10%
Scenario A           3     12   12    12
Scenario B          10     16   16    16
Scenario C          12     20   20    20

Interpretation
--------------
The Pareto result depends materially on the deployment objectives.

A signing-centric analysis yields a compact three-configuration front,
but adding key-generation cost substantially expands the nondominated
set. Adding verification cost expands it further.

Under Scenario C, no configuration improves all six included objectives
by at least 2% relative to another configuration. Consequently all
20 configurations survive the 2%, 5%, and 10% robust-dominance
sensitivity tests.

These configurations represent trade-offs and must not be interpreted
as an ordered ranking or as a single universally optimal parameter set.
